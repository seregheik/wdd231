WDD 231 Project Site Plan

Street Eats

A guide to street foods from around the world

Site Name

Street Eats — suggested domain: streeteatsguide.com

The name is short, easy to remember, and tells visitors exactly what the site is about: food that is sold and eaten on the street. Adding "guide" to the domain makes it clear that the site helps people discover and learn about dishes, and it keeps the domain unique and easy to spell.

Site Purpose

Street Eats helps food lovers discover, learn about, and cook popular street foods from different countries. The site will provide:

  • A home page with a featured "Dish of the Day" and an introduction to street-food culture.
  • An Explore page listing at least fifteen dishes, each with its name, country of origin, main ingredients, spice level, preparation time, and photo. Visitors can filter dishes by region or spice level, open a detailed view of any dish, and save favorites for their next visit.
  • A Share a Dish page with a form where visitors suggest a street food from their own city, followed by a confirmation page that displays what they submitted.

Scenarios

These questions from the target audience — curious home cooks, travelers, and food fans — will guide the site's content:

  1. "I'm traveling to Thailand next month. What street foods should I try?"

    The Explore page lets the visitor filter dishes by region and read a short description of each dish so they know what to look for.

  2. "I want to cook something new this weekend that isn't too spicy and doesn't take hours."

    Each dish lists its spice level and preparation time, and the detail view shows the main ingredients and cooking steps.

  3. "My city has an amazing snack nobody knows about. How can I share it?"

    The Share a Dish form lets visitors submit their own favorite street food and confirms what they sent.

Color Scheme

Warm, appetizing colors inspired by chili peppers, spices, and market stalls.

  • Chili Red #A4161A

    Primary color: headings, header and footer backgrounds, buttons, and links.

  • Saffron #F4A300

    Accent color: highlights, card borders, focus outlines, and the "Dish of the Day" badge. Never used for text on light backgrounds.

  • Cream #FFF8EE

    Page background and text on Chili Red backgrounds.

  • Charcoal #2B2B2B

    Body text.

Typography

Poppins (Bold 700)

Grilled, fried, and wrapped to go

Used for the site name, headings, and buttons. Its rounded, geometric shapes feel friendly and bold.

Nunito Sans (Regular 400, Bold 700)

Used for body text, dish descriptions, and form labels. It is clean and very readable at small sizes on mobile screens.

Wireframes

Hand-drawn layout of the home page for small and large screens.
